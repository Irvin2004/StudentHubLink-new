# StudentHub Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in StudentHub welcoming, respectful,
safe, and inclusive for everyone, regardless of experience, education, identity,
background, ability, appearance, or personal circumstances.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, constructive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports the StudentHub community include:

- treating other people with respect;
- welcoming questions and new contributors;
- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- receiving feedback professionally;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting different experiences and viewpoints;
- focusing discussion on the project and its users;
- protecting private student and contributor information;
- crediting the work and ideas of others; and
- prioritizing the safety and accessibility of the community.

Technical disagreement is normal. Explain trade-offs with evidence, ask
questions, and criticize ideas rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, or intimidation;
- discriminatory language or conduct;
- personal attacks, insults, or deliberately humiliating comments;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling or repeatedly disrupting productive discussion;
- spam, impersonation, or deceptive participation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sharing private messages, student records, credentials, or security-report
  details;
- encouraging others to violate this policy; and
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all StudentHub project spaces, including:

- issues and pull requests;
- code review;
- discussions and project boards;
- documentation and source code;
- community chat or meetings operated by the project; and
- public spaces where a person is representing StudentHub.

It also applies to maintainers when they use an official project account or
otherwise represent the project.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
behavior. They may edit, hide, reject, or remove comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, documentation, or other contributions that violate this policy.

Maintainers should explain moderation decisions when doing so is safe and
appropriate. Reports must be handled as consistently, promptly, and privately as
possible.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to the
[repository maintainer](https://github.com/Irvin2004). If direct private contact
is unavailable, ask for a private communication channel without publishing
sensitive details or allegations.

Include:

- what happened;
- where and when it happened;
- relevant links or screenshots;
- whether the behavior is continuing;
- immediate safety concerns; and
- the outcome you are requesting, if any.

Do not use the public issue tracker to disclose private information or detailed
allegations.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent practical.
Information may be shared only with people required to investigate, protect
participants, or comply with the law.

Anyone reviewing a report must disclose a conflict of interest and step aside
when necessary.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may consider context, impact, intent, history, and willingness to
correct the behavior. Actions may include:

1. **Correction** — A private or public request to stop or edit inappropriate
   behavior.
2. **Warning** — A formal warning describing the violation and consequences of
   repetition.
3. **Temporary restriction** — Temporary limits on participation or contact with
   affected community members.
4. **Permanent removal** — Permanent exclusion from project spaces for serious
   or repeated violations.

Severe threats, harassment, exposure of private information, or deliberate
security harm may result in immediate permanent removal.

## Appeals

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration by
providing relevant context or evidence privately. An appeal does not pause
measures needed to protect participants.

## No retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern, provides evidence, or
participates in an investigation in good faith is prohibited.

## Project focus

StudentHub exists to help students collaborate and learn. Healthy contribution
means leaving the project and its community safer, clearer, and more useful than
we found them.
